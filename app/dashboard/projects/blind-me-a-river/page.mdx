I tried to created a indie horror game called Blind Me A River with my friends. Teaching me skills about project managment and deligating task along with the pains of scope creep.

Reasons:
Wanting to get into game development/ become a better coder
Improve my management abilities 
Fun?

## Short Story Description:
I wanted to make a small scale project with my friends. We were all excited to do such a project and I had prior experience with development, so I would drive the project and direct and what should be achieved from the project which was a complete horror game.

We wanted to build a simple game that was appealing to each of us while also being thought as a feasible project that could be achieved within a valid period of time. This was an important factor as this would decide our overall commitment towards the project and at the end having a full on project is more worth while then nothing to show for.

The main focus was having a blind character as the player character and entieties that are looked at would cause indiviauls to go insane and due to our chacarter being blind they are uneffected. But this information is unknown to our character and they would have to slowly discover what happened to the world and learn to adjust with this new world of horror.

That is just a general overview and the story is more fleshed out with the world of mystery.

## Managing the project
### Ideation, planning & Roles
The idea was to establish a ground level group and build out a plan of the project and with the plan we would make use of the "Agile Methodology" where we would have biweekly meeting on the progression of the project. 

Within the planning process we would have a open road map, this would not have specific points but give a general idea of what each stage would look like to help drive and deliver the project. 

I would function as the scrum master and project lead having the meetings that directed the projects vision. I deligated tasks to people with their respective skills this active information would be recoreded down in a discord pinned message saying the goal of each person. Along with that I managed resourcesing such as where people will store content and who to question on the decision making process.

I  was the developer of the project, because I have an intrest within the development process, and prior experience. This experience was helpful and efficient as I understand the direction of how the project would flow and I had a great amount of control over the project and what would need to be done.

Other roles that were taken up by my team:
* **Story designer:** They were in charage of story development along with the plot of our story
* **Level Designer/ 3d Modeller:** - The level designer would take the story idea and would design the level schematics of what would be built along with Modelling those designs out within the leveling process along with any models
* **3d Modeller:** - This person would create the 3d models for the designs of the project

### Unity
With the project I made use of a  game development platform called unity [Unity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unity_(game_engine)) this was a helpful tool within the game development process due to it long maturity and the fact that there was a large amount of information provided on its use and how it functions. I looked at various tutorials and documentation that existed on how to use the platform which was very helpful.

Within the project I would apply the knowledge and tools towards the. One aspect that was interesting was the use of the echo location system that I made to help allow the blind character to navigate across the world. The idea was that sounds would help trigger a graph effect that would allow the player to see since they are technically blind and that would make it difficult to navigate around the environment. I applied the use of the shader graph system to create a visual effect that would allow the player to on click to see the environment that they were in before their screen turned pitch black again allowing it to highlight the surrounding area.

Along with that I had an intresting time trying to create the logic for the player movement. While the idea is that a player is able to move left, right up and down. There are subtle factors that go into every movement system to make it immersive as possible such as simple bobbing or smoothness along with how the camera interacts with the player as they are moving which is extremely interesting and I was unaware of such factors until I started making the movement and getting more comfortable with it. In future I would like to improve the movement system that I create and make it more entertained and hopefully in future I can create the asset and reuse it within future projects. Because movement is extremely fun and very unique within different games.

However I did face problems with the unity systems, while it is a mature system. The team who builds the service has made new additions which has effect some aspect of the information that was provided on the way that unity works and the tooling that exists. Which was frustrating at points where information from specific tutorials applying specific methods could not be so easily replicated. While the documentation was handy to get over those certain problems it still was a headache trying to resolve issues.

## Why the project failed
While the project was great and it was extremely fun at the end it did fail to meet expectations that we had with the game.

Reason why
* Scope Creep
* Timelines
* Lack of skill

### Scope Creep
The idea of [scope creep](https://www.apm.org.uk/blog/what-is-scope-creep-and-how-can-we-mitigate-it/) is the concept of having a set goal but overtime you add more and more to that goal preventing you from being able to finish the project. Throughout the project we were in a position where we were happy with the project at various stages and were we like it would be cool if we added this or changed that, but this put added pressure on us to complete the overall task and increase the amount of time it took to complete project.
### Timelines
With the added effect of scope creep we never really had a defined timeline on when we would complete various tasks. This would be a problem as there wouldn't be a set direct incentive on trying to deliver a certain task on time, because there was no time so it would put people in a situation where they would deal with it later and this would be a problem. 

In future I should try to implement a road map to define a specific time scale on where various goals would be reach for example character models would be build on may 7th and then level sets would be built on June 3rd. 

This factor in combination with the fact that we had to all balance our own personal lives and deadline such as university work added on further pressure and time.
### Lack of skill
Finally the factor that did kill the game was our over confidence and the lack of skill that we had to complete such a game. This was one of the biggest factor as this such project was our first game to make, which would be quite intense especially as we were only doing this as a hobby. While we got better overtime this did not make up for prior mistakes or problems that were faced throughout the project and my lack of development skills within game development did show through and prevented me from being able to successfully deliver the tasks along with various other factors.
